About GameAccountValue

A free calculator on this site and an AI appraisal bot in Telegram, built on dated, checkable market data.

What the service does

  • On this site: a free calculator for each of 9 games gives a rough range right in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
  • In the bot: one free estimate a day from a single screenshot.
  • Paid full audit (2–3 screenshots, paid in Telegram Stars): a price range with confidence and liquidity, a numbered PDF collector card with QR verification and a short video.

Where the numbers come from

Price ranges are built from live marketplace listings that are checked by hand and dated on every page. They are asking prices, not confirmed sales, so every result is a range, not a quote. An item is named as a value driver only if it appears in two or more independent top-price listings. The full rules are on the methodology page.

AI tools help with research, translation and code; the rules on this page decide what reaches you.

What we never do

  • Ask for game logins or passwords.
  • Store your screenshots.
  • Run ads, trackers or cookies on this site.
  • Scrape marketplaces automatically — listings are checked by a person.

How the project is paid for

Only by paid audits in the bot, paid in Telegram Stars. Today no place in any ranking or estimate on this site is paid for; if a sponsored link ever appears, it will be clearly labelled as sponsored.

Found a mistake?

Prices move and translations can slip. If you see a wrong number, an outdated fact or a clumsy translation, write to @GameAccountValue_Bot — the page gets corrected and its date badge updated.
