How much is a Brawl Stars account worth?

Market range: $3 – $150 (up to $300+ for top accounts)

Live listings on Eldorado.gg run from $3 (12,000 trophies, 55 brawlers) up to $35 for the site's top-shown accounts (40,000+ trophies, 100+ brawlers). A larger aggregate market (igitems.com, 1,147 active listings) shows most accounts actually trade between $15-$150, with a 90-day average asking price of $73-101, and a top bracket of 34 listings over $300.

What actually determines a Brawl Stars account's value?

Checked directly against the 10 highest-priced live listings: none of them named a specific rare skin at all. Every one of the top 10 was titled purely by trophy count and brawler count (e.g. "104 BRAWLERS🔥51K🏆") — unlike Fortnite, Genshin, or Mobile Legends, Brawl Stars' resale value doesn't concentrate around owning particular cosmetics. The real drivers are:

  • Trophy count — 30,000+ is a common threshold for "high-end" listings
  • Brawlers at Power Level 11 with Hypercharge unlocked
  • Gem balance and Brawl Pass completion history
  • Global leaderboard ranking

How can I make my own account more valuable?

  • Push trophies steadily on brawlers you already have maxed rather than spreading progress thin
  • Prioritize limited-time skins during events — they stop being obtainable once the event ends
  • Complete the seasonal Brawl Pass each season rather than skipping seasons, since some cosmetics don't return

What are the biggest scams in Brawl Stars account trading?

Fake "escrow" middlemen who take payment and never deliver login credentials, and chargeback fraud where a buyer pays, receives the account, then reverses the payment through their bank or PayPal after the seller has already handed over access. Both are common enough that most established marketplaces now push their own buyer/seller protection instead of direct peer deals.

Is trading Brawl Stars accounts against the rules?

Yes — Supercell's Terms of Service prohibit selling, buying, or transferring accounts. Accounts confirmed to have changed hands can be permanently banned, and Supercell support does not restore access lost in a trade scam.

Where do these listings actually come from?

Eldorado.gg and igitems.com both host public seller listings with asking prices — these are the closest thing to real market data available, though they're asking prices, not confirmed completed sales. igitems also separately tracks sold-price movement, which recently ran 6.8% above asking despite falling ask prices — a sign real buyers are still paying up even as sellers list more cautiously.

Player guide: how do you actually push trophies faster?

This section is for improving your own play, not your account's resale value. A July 2026 guide from TrophyCoach recommends locking onto one Brawler per session rather than switching around, since win-streak bonuses reset per-Brawler; winning the fight for lane control in the first 15 seconds of a match; keeping at least one shot/ammo in reserve unless you have a confirmed kill or objective; and — specifically for beginners — pushing several Brawlers to a moderate 300-500 trophies rather than pouring everything into one Brawler past 900.

A single-source tier list (mumuplayer.com, not independently cross-confirmed) names Colette, Damian, Edgar, Crow, Otis, Chester, Starr Nova, Max, and Surge as the July 2026 S-tier — treat this list with more caution than the sourced tips above. On the esports side, HMBLE won back-to-back Brawl Cup titles in 2026, and Crazy Raccoon were the 2025 World Champions. Brawl Stars isn't an aim-based game, so there's no meaningful sensitivity-setting culture around it the way there is for shooters.
