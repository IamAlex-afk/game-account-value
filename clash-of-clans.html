How much is a Clash of Clans account worth?

Market range: $150 – $700+

A fully developed Town Hall 16 with max-level heroes typically sells for $150-$500; accounts with a strong Legend League history and large spell stockpiles exceed $700.

What actually determines a Clash of Clans account's value?

  • Town Hall level and how complete the hero upgrades are for that level
  • Legend League history and season trophy counts
  • Builder Base progress (secondary, but adds value)
  • Spell and siege machine stockpiles
  • Clan reputation and War Star history

How can I make my own account more valuable?

  • Finish hero upgrades before rushing the next Town Hall level — a maxed TH14 reads as more valuable than a half-upgraded TH16
  • Push into Legend League consistently rather than occasionally, since season history compounds
  • Keep gold pass / season pass active each season for the exclusive cosmetics that don't return

What are the biggest scams in Clash of Clans account trading?

Fake payment proof screenshots, "verify your account" phishing links sent through Discord that steal Supercell ID credentials, and buyers who dispute the payment with their bank after receiving login access. Because Supercell doesn't mediate account trades, there is no dispute process if either side gets scammed.

Is trading Clash of Clans accounts against the rules?

Yes — Supercell's Terms of Service prohibit selling, buying, or transferring accounts, and violations can result in a permanent ban with no recovery.

Where do these listings actually come from?

Third-party marketplaces such as PlayerAuctions, Eldorado, EB24, and Skycoach host public seller listings with asking prices — the closest available approximation of real market data, though not confirmed completed sales.
