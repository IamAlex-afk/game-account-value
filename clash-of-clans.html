Real Market Prices

How much is a Clash of Clans account worth?

Clash of Clans is now on Town Hall 18, and account price scales directly with both Town Hall level and how "rushed" (upgraded unevenly, skipping defenses/heroes) vs. "full max" the account is. A rushed TH13 sells for around $10, while a genuinely full-max TH18 lists for $100-$260.

Does this range hold up on a different type of marketplace?

Yes, roughly. EpicNPC — a peer-to-peer trading forum rather than a storefront marketplace like Eldorado.gg or igitems.com — shows individual TH17/TH18 listings priced from around $98 up to $340, checked via indexed search results in September 2026 (EpicNPC blocks direct automated page access, so these are the asking prices as surfaced by search indexing, not a page we fetched and quoted directly). That's the same general shape as the igitems.com table above — TH17/TH18 full-max accounts in the low hundreds — with EpicNPC's highest individual listing sitting somewhat above igitems.com's $260 ceiling, consistent with the fact that a forum lets a seller name any asking price rather than compete against a storefront's other listings.

What Actually Determines Value

What actually determines a Clash of Clans account's value?

  • Town Hall level and how complete the hero upgrades are for that level
  • Legend League history and season trophy counts
  • Builder Base progress (secondary, but adds value)
  • Spell and siege machine stockpiles
  • Clan reputation and War Star history

How can I make my own account more valuable?

  • Finish hero and defense upgrades before rushing the next Town Hall level — the tier table above shows a full-max account is worth roughly 2-4x more than a rushed account at the same Town Hall
  • Push into Legend League consistently rather than occasionally, since season history compounds
  • Keep gold pass / season pass active each season for the exclusive cosmetics that don't return

What an estimate can and can't tell you

The calculator above and the bot both turn what they can see into a price range. Here is where that range stands on firm ground and where it doesn't.

✅ Where it holds up

  • It rests on a large sample: 4,463 igitems.com listings, broken out by Town Hall level and upgrade progress (September 2026), with Eldorado.gg and EpicNPC listings showing the same general shape.
  • Town Hall level and how complete the base is are the two biggest price drivers in that data — a full-max TH18 lists for $100–$260, a rushed one for $20–$35.
  • The quick calculator asks only those two things. The bot's screenshot check also reads hero levels, epic equipment, hero skins, sceneries, CWL medals and Builder Hall level.

⚠️ Where it falls short

  • These are asking prices, not completed sales: listings show what sellers want, not what buyers finally paid.
  • Asking prices are not always logical. At TH17, rushed accounts are listed at up to $55 — above the $50 top of standard ones. The table shows the market as it is, without smoothing it out.
  • A snapshot ages. The figures are from September 2026 and shift with every Town Hall release and balance update.
  • A price is not a permission. Supercell's Terms of Service prohibit selling or transferring accounts, and a traded account can be banned for good — the range describes the market, it does not say anyone could or should sell.

Scams & Trading Risks

What are the biggest scams in Clash of Clans account trading?

Fake payment proof screenshots, "verify your account" phishing links sent through Discord that steal Supercell ID credentials, and buyers who dispute the payment with their bank after receiving login access. Because Supercell doesn't mediate account trades, there is no dispute process if either side gets scammed.

Rules & Trading Policy

Is trading Clash of Clans accounts against the rules?

Yes — Supercell's Terms of Service prohibit selling, buying, or transferring accounts, and violations can result in a permanent ban with no recovery.

Where do these listings actually come from?

igitems.com's live market report (4,463 listings, broken out by Town Hall level and progression state) and Eldorado.gg's public listings are the sources for the numbers above — real asking prices, not confirmed completed sales.

Player Guide

Player guide: what upgrade order do experienced players actually recommend?

This section is for improving your own base, not your account's resale value. A 2026 upgrade-priority framework published by ClashOS and ClashVault summarizes the commonly-recommended order as: Laboratory first, then Army Camps and Clan Castle, then Hero Hall/Blacksmith, then core defenses, with walls deliberately last — the shorthand both guides use is "offense first, time bottlenecks second, defenses third, walls last." Base-layout and upgrade-guide content from creator Judo Sloth Gaming covers the same 2026 priority approach in more detail.

Clash of Clans isn't an aim-based game, so there's no meaningful sensitivity-setting culture around it the way there is for shooters.
