Notable Findings

What's the biggest current risk, beyond price?

Recovery scam — the single most-cited risk

The original seller reclaims the account after payment by going to Supercell Support with the original linked email, device history, and purchase receipts. This works regardless of how carefully the buyer changes passwords, because Supercell identifies the "real" owner by account-creation data the buyer never controls.

Trading is a ToS violation for both sides

Supercell explicitly reserves the right to ban any account that has been transferred, bought, sold, or shared — account sharing/"pushing" can get both the sharer's and the borrower's accounts banned, often permanently.

In-game trading is effectively dead as of 2026: Trade Tokens became unobtainable around October 2025, so any third party currently claiming to sell Trade Tokens, gems, or cards is running a scam by definition. No unique record-sale item was found — value here is driven by aggregate collection completeness, not one standout rare item.
