Real Market Prices

How much is a Clash Royale account worth?

Live listings on Eldorado.gg run from $0.50 for a starter account up to $600 for a "full max" account. A larger aggregate market (igitems.com, 852 listings) shows the average asking price recently moved from $81.89 to $107.20 — up nearly 31% — while sold prices ran about 21% below asking, meaning buyers are negotiating hard even as sellers ask for more.

Does a forum-based marketplace show different numbers than the storefront sites above?

Mostly the same range, with one clear outlier at the top. EpicNPC — a player-to-player trading forum, distinct from Eldorado.gg and igitems.com — has listings that sit inside this page's own brackets, like a KT16 account with 63 maxed cards at $150. But it also carries a listing well above this page's $300 top bracket: an "OG 2019" account with 30,000+ gems, a rare 2019 badge combo, 15 Level 16 cards, and 21 evolutions, asking $1,500 — found via indexed search results (EpicNPC blocks direct automated page access, so these are asking prices as surfaced by search indexing, not a page we fetched and quoted directly ourselves). Account age and a rare original-cosmetic combination, not just King Tower level, is what pushes a listing that far above the typical top bracket.

What Actually Determines Value

What actually determines a Clash Royale account's value?

Checked directly against the 10 highest-priced live listings: 9 of them named zero specific cosmetics. Titles were built entirely around King Tower level, trophy count, and evolution count (e.g. "KT 16🏆13K🏆14 EVO+(4 OSK)🔮60 Emotes"). Only one listing named a specific emote ("Goblin Kiss Trophy"), a single mention, not a repeated pattern. The real drivers are:

  • King Tower level and how complete the card level upgrades are
  • Trophy count and ranking on the ladder / Legendary Path
  • Number of evolution cards unlocked
  • Tournament/challenge win history
  • Card collection completeness across all rarities

What should I look up in my account before using the calculator?

  • The calculator asks for: King Tower level, Max-level cards. Look these up in Clash Royale before you start.
  • Which of these moves the price most for Clash Royale is explained in the list above.
  • The result is a rough range of public asking prices, not an offer. Buying or selling accounts breaks Supercell's terms — see the rules.

What an estimate can and can't tell you

The calculator above and the bot both turn what they can see into a price range. Here is where that range stands on firm ground and where it doesn’t.

✅ Where it holds up

  • It rests on real listings: Eldorado.gg (from $0.50 for a starter account to $600 for a full max one) and 852 igitems.com listings broken into price brackets (September 2026).
  • In the 10 highest-priced listings checked, 9 named no cosmetics at all — titles were built on King Tower level, trophies and evolutions, the same things the estimate is built on.
  • The quick calculator asks only King Tower level and max-level cards. The bot’s screenshot check also reads evolutions, champions, heroes, tower skins, rare emotes, trophies, gems, Pass Royale, a CRL badge and global tournament wins.

⚠️ Where it falls short

  • These are asking prices. On igitems.com sold prices ran about 21% below asking — what a buyer finally pays is usually lower than the listing.
  • The market moves fast: the average asking price there rose from $81.89 to $107.20 in a few months. The figures are from September 2026 and change with every season and balance update.
  • Two accounts at the same King Tower level can differ a lot in value: evolutions and card levels matter, and the quick calculator cannot see them.
  • A price is not a permission. Supercell’s Terms of Service prohibit selling or transferring accounts, and a traded account can be banned for good — the range describes the market, it does not say anyone could or should sell.

Scams & Trading Risks

What are the biggest scams in Clash Royale account trading?

Fake escrow services on Discord that vanish after payment, and "trial before you buy" scams where a seller lets a buyer log in temporarily then changes the password back and disappears with the funds. Supercell does not mediate or reverse either kind of loss.

Rules & Trading Policy

Is trading Clash Royale accounts against the rules?

Yes — Supercell's Terms of Service prohibit account sales and transfers, and detected trades can result in a permanent, unappealable ban.

Where do these listings actually come from?

Eldorado.gg and igitems.com host public seller listings with asking prices — the closest available approximation of real market pricing, though asking prices rather than confirmed sales.

Player Guide

Player guide: what's the current meta, and who actually plays at the top level?

This section is for improving your own play, not your account's resale value. As of August 2026, RoyaleAPI's live ladder data and community guides (Gaming ProMax, TrophyCoach) cite Evo Royal Giant Fisherman (~56% win rate) and PEKKA Bridge Spam (~61%+ in the top 1,000) as leading decks, alongside Hog Cycle, Goblin Barrel Bait, and Elite Barbarians Evolution; the "Hog 2.6 Cycle" archetype has stayed part of the meta continuously since 2017. Two real balance changes reshaped the meta this year: a June 2026 patch cut crown-tower damage on Fireball/Rocket/Arrows/Poison/Log (weakening spell-chip decks), and an August 2026 patch nerfed all three Spirit cards so none can solo a tower.

The highest-earning competitive player is Mohamed Light, a 3-time CRL World Champion (2022, 2024, 2025) with roughly $960,000 in career earnings. Clash Royale is a touchscreen tap-timing game rather than an aim-based one, so sensitivity settings simply aren't part of its competitive culture.
