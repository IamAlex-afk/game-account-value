Notable Findings

What's the biggest current risk, beyond price?

Concrete 2026 scam incidents

Reported (Jan 2026): an account was unlinked from Facebook with the email changed without permission post-sale. Reported (Apr 2026): a buyer who paid ~800 INR via PhonePe for an account that was hacked shortly after. These are dated, specific incidents, not generic warnings.

Facebook-link single point of failure

Free Fire accounts are commonly linked via Facebook login, so the account's security is only as strong as the linked Facebook account — if that Facebook account is phished, the Free Fire account goes with it.

The rarest known items match the "OG account" pricing pattern above: pre-OB20 (2017–2019) one-time event exclusives that were never reprinted, like the Sakura Blossom bundle (first-ever Elite Pass, 2018) and Galaxy Dino skins. Garena's zero-tolerance anti-cheat policy (reaffirmed effective June 9, 2026) states bans under it are never reversible — relevant since an account with boosting history carries real retroactive-ban risk.
