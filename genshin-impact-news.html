Notable Findings

What's the biggest current risk, beyond price?

Recovery is conditional on clean payment history

HoYoverse can restore a hacked or disputed account if ownership is proven — but recovery support explicitly gets harder or fails if the account has illegal/unauthorized top-ups on it. Many traded accounts are inflated precisely via unauthorized third-party top-up services, which undermines the buyer's ability to get support help later.

"Primogem generator" phishing

A Genshin-specific scam pattern: sites/DMs that clone real HoYoverse login or top-up pages to harvest credentials (and sometimes deliver malware) rather than actually granting free currency.

Streamer "Razerninjas" remains the most-cited documented whale account (over $100,000 spent, every weapon at max refinement) — this is a separate collector-spending case, not a marketplace sale price, and is kept as its own category rather than merged into the pricing above. A US age-verification requirement (effective from May 20, 2025, continuing through 2026) is relevant to trading since a bought/sold account may need re-verification under the new "owner"'s identity.
