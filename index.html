Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my Roblox account value?

Open @GameAccountValue_Bot on Telegram, select Roblox, then either try the free 1-screenshot estimate or pay with Telegram Stars for the full 2-3 screenshot audit. Send screenshots of your avatar inventory and Robux balance — the AI returns a market value estimate, and paid audits include a PDF certificate.

How do I find out how much my Brawl Stars account is worth?

Use @GameAccountValue_Bot on Telegram. Select Brawl Stars, send up to 3 screenshots of your profile, brawler list, and skins. The AI analyzes your trophy count, brawler collection, and rare skins to estimate market value.

How much is my Clash of Clans account worth?

Open @GameAccountValue_Bot, select Clash of Clans, and send screenshots of your Town Hall level, hero levels, and gem balance. The AI checks for a maxed Town Hall, hero skins, and CWL medals to estimate market value.

How do I find out my Clash Royale account value?

Use @GameAccountValue_Bot, select Clash Royale, and send screenshots of your King Tower level, card collection, and tower skins. The AI checks max/evolution cards and champion cards to estimate value.

How much is my Free Fire account worth?

Open @GameAccountValue_Bot, select Free Fire, and send screenshots of your diamond balance, EVO weapon upgrades, and character roster. The AI checks for rare bundles and premium characters like DJ Alok or Chrono.

How do I check my Genshin Impact account value?

Use @GameAccountValue_Bot, select Genshin Impact, and send screenshots of your Adventure Rank, character list, and weapon inventory. The AI checks 5-star character constellations (C6) and weapon refinements (R5).

How much is my Mobile Legends account worth?

Open @GameAccountValue_Bot, select Mobile Legends, and send screenshots of your rank, hero collection, and skins. The AI checks for Collector and Legend skins and Starlight membership.

How do I find out my Fortnite account value?

Use @GameAccountValue_Bot, select Fortnite, and send screenshots of your locker and V-Bucks balance. The AI checks for OG Chapter 1 skins like Renegade Raider or Black Knight, the most valuable items in the game.

How much is my Minecraft account worth?

Open @GameAccountValue_Bot, select Minecraft, and send a screenshot of your profile. The AI checks account type (Java vs Bedrock), username length, and rare capes to estimate value.

Do you need my account password?

Never. You only send screenshots — the same images you'd share in any Discord listing. No login, no credentials, no risk.

How accurate is the valuation?

The AI identifies high-value items (limiteds, rare skins, max heroes) using a deterministic pricing formula per game. Results are a range, not a single number — treat it as an independent reference estimate, not a market quote.

What's the difference between the free and paid estimate?

Free (1/day): send 1 screenshot, get a quick rough tier — enough to see the bot actually works. Paid: send 2-3 screenshots (the AI sees your full inventory, not just one screen), get a price range plus a PDF certificate with a QR verification code. The free tier is intentionally rougher — it can miss rare items that only show up in a 2nd or 3rd screenshot.

How do I pay?

Via Telegram Stars (XTR) — the official Telegram payment system. Safe, instant, built into the app. Price adjusts by region.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the AI cannot produce a valuation due to unclear screenshots or a technical error, Stars are automatically refunded.

What exactly is this service?

GameAccountValue is an entertainment and information service that estimates the approximate value invested in a game collection. We are NOT a marketplace. We do NOT sell, buy, or facilitate account transfers. Account trading may violate game rules — always follow each game's Terms of Service.
