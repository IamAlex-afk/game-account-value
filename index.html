Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need my account password?

Never. You only send screenshots — the same images you'd share in any Discord listing. No login, no credentials, no risk.

How accurate is the valuation?

The AI identifies high-value items (limiteds, rare skins, max heroes) using a deterministic pricing formula per game. Results are a range, not a single number — treat it as an independent reference estimate, not a market quote.

What's the difference between the free and paid estimate?

Free (1/day): send 1 screenshot, get a quick rough tier — enough to see the bot actually works. Paid: send 2-3 screenshots (the AI sees your full inventory, not just one screen), get a price range plus a PDF certificate with a QR verification code. The free tier is intentionally rougher — it can miss rare items that only show up in a 2nd or 3rd screenshot.

How do I pay?

Via Telegram Stars (XTR) — the official Telegram payment system. Safe, instant, built into the app. Price adjusts by region.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the AI cannot produce a valuation due to unclear screenshots or a technical error, Stars are automatically refunded.
