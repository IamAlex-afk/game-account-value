⛏️ Minecraft

📅 Full launch November 18, 2011 👥 ~212M monthly active players (2026) 📦 350M+ copies sold — the best-selling video game of all time (confirmed by Mojang)

How much is a Minecraft account worth?

Minecraft is the one game here with a genuinely public, long-running resale market — mostly for the username itself, not in-game progress.

What's the most valuable Minecraft account on record?

Community marketplace reports
Two-letter usernames (e.g. "eu") have reportedly sold for $50,000+, two-character alphanumeric combos around $25,000+, and the rarest two-digit numeric usernames (e.g. "07") have been reported near $200,000 — treat that top figure as an extreme outlier, not a typical price. Accounts with an original Minecon 2011 cape list for $2,000–$5,000+.

What makes a Minecraft account valuable?

Username length and word-likeness (no numbers), legacy capes, and original (pre-Microsoft-migration) account status.
