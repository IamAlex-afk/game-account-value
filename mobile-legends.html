🏆 Mobile Legends Account Value

No single "record sale" exists for Mobile Legends — here's what resale listings actually show, and what drives the price up or down.

Updated September 2026. Sources listed at the bottom of the page.

📅 Released July 14, 2016 (Android), November 2016 (iOS) 👥 40-110M monthly active players (estimates vary widely by source) 🌍 Strongest in Indonesia, Philippines, Malaysia — dominant across Southeast Asia 📦 1.5B+ installs across 139 countries

How much is a Mobile Legends account worth?

We found no independently-verified record sale for a Mobile Legends account. A resale market does exist on G2G, PlayerAuctions, and FunPay, where prices are typically driven by skin rarity rather than a single standout figure — unlike Brawl Stars or Clash Royale, there's no widely-cited overall price range for MLBB accounts.

What actually determines a Mobile Legends account's value?

  • Epic and Legend-class skin collection — especially retired collector skins
  • Rank (Mythical Glory and above)
  • Hero collection completeness — owning all or nearly all heroes
  • Emblem levels and talent point investment
  • Account region/server, since some regions have thinner resale demand than others

Why doesn't Mobile Legends have a clear price range like other games in this report?

Because value is driven almost entirely by which specific skins an account owns rather than a single progress metric like Town Hall level — two accounts with identical rank can differ by hundreds of dollars in value if one owns a retired collector skin and the other doesn't. That makes a single "typical range" much less meaningful than for games with a clearer progression ladder.

How can I make my own account more valuable?

  • Buy limited-time collector skins during their release window — most never return, and resale demand tracks rarity closely
  • Push rank consistently each season rather than letting it decay, since Mythical Glory history carries weight
  • Complete hero collection breadth before investing heavily in cosmetics for just a few heroes

What are the biggest scams in Mobile Legends account trading?

Fake middlemen on Facebook/Discord groups who take payment for an account and never deliver credentials, and "rent-to-buy" scams where a buyer is given temporary access, pays, then the seller changes the linked email and locks them out. Moonton does not mediate account-trading disputes.

Is trading Mobile Legends accounts against the rules?

Yes — Moonton's Terms of Service prohibit account sales and transfers, and confirmed trading can result in a permanent ban.

Where do these listings actually come from?

Marketplaces such as G2G, PlayerAuctions, and FunPay host public seller listings — the closest available approximation of real market activity, though asking prices rather than confirmed sales.
