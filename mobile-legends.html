Real Market Prices

How much is a Mobile Legends account worth?

Live listings on Eldorado.gg run from $0.50 (fresh Level 3 smurf accounts) to $250 for a 605-skin "mega collector" account. A larger aggregate market (igitems.com, 1,147 listings) confirms a real distribution: current average asking price is $68.61, and their own buying guide states a "solid account" with 300-400 skins runs $200-$300, while top collector accounts with 800+ cosmetics sell for $1,500+. In Indonesia — Mobile Legends' single biggest market — Tokopedia listings ("jual akun mobile legends") run from Rp80,000 to Rp9,570,000 (~$5 to ~$605).

What Actually Determines Value

What actually determines a Mobile Legends account's value?

  • Epic and Legend-class skin collection — especially retired collector skins like the one above
  • Rank (Mythical Glory and above)
  • Hero collection completeness — owning all or nearly all heroes
  • Emblem levels and talent point investment
  • Account region/server, since some regions have thinner resale demand than others

Why does Mobile Legends pricing vary so much between accounts at the same rank?

Value is driven heavily by which specific skins an account owns, not just rank — two accounts with identical Mythical Glory rank can differ by hundreds of dollars if one owns retired collector skins and the other doesn't. That's why the price brackets above have wide overlap between tiers rather than a clean step-up like Clash of Clans' Town Hall table.

How can I make my own account more valuable?

  • Buy limited-time collector skins during their release window — most never return, and resale demand tracks rarity closely
  • Push rank consistently each season rather than letting it decay, since Mythical Glory history carries weight
  • Complete hero collection breadth before investing heavily in cosmetics for just a few heroes

Scams & Trading Risks

What are the biggest scams in Mobile Legends account trading?

Fake middlemen on Facebook/Discord groups who take payment for an account and never deliver credentials, and "rent-to-buy" scams where a buyer is given temporary access, pays, then the seller changes the linked email and locks them out. Moonton does not mediate account-trading disputes.

Rules & Trading Policy

Is trading Mobile Legends accounts against the rules?

Yes — Moonton's Terms of Service prohibit account sales and transfers, and confirmed trading can result in a permanent ban.

Player guide: is there a real "pro settings" culture, and what's the current meta?

This section is for improving your own play, not your account's resale value.
