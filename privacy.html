Privacy Policy

← Back

GameAccountValue processes screenshots in RAM only. Images are never stored on disk by us. User Telegram IDs are SHA-256 hashed before database storage. We do not sell your data. Designed in line with the principles of GDPR (EU) 2016/679, including data minimization, purpose limitation, and the right to erasure (see /delete).

Third-Party Processing

To generate a valuation, your screenshots are sent to Google's Gemini API for AI vision analysis at the moment you submit them. This is a necessary part of how the service works — we cannot estimate a value without it. Google processes this data under its own API terms and privacy policy; we do not control Google's retention practices for API inputs. No other third party receives your screenshots.

Data We Collect

  • Hashed Telegram user ID
  • Game type selected
  • Timestamp of valuation
  • AI-generated valuation result

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Screenshots — held in RAM only for the duration of the Gemini API call described above, then discarded; never written to disk or retained by us
  • Account credentials or passwords
  • Payment details (handled by Telegram)

Legal Basis for Processing

We process the data listed above under GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) — it's necessary to perform the valuation you asked for. Where you use paid features, processing your payment status is also necessary for that contract.

Retention

We keep the data listed above (hashed ID, game type, timestamp, valuation result) until you delete it yourself via the /delete command — there is no automatic expiry. /delete removes it immediately and completely, including any partner/referral balance.

Automated Decision-Making

Your valuation is generated entirely by automated AI processing (see Third-Party Processing above) — no human reviews or approves individual results. This does not produce a legal or similarly significant effect on you: it's an entertainment estimate, not a binding price, credit decision, or eligibility determination, so it does not trigger the additional rights under GDPR Art. 22. See the AI Disclaimer in our Terms of Service for how to interpret the result.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to our processing of your data, and to receive a copy of it in a portable format. Given the data we hold is limited to a hashed ID, game type, timestamp, and valuation result, most of these are exercised the same way: contact us or use /delete. If you're in the EU/EEA/UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority at any time.
