GameAccountValue processes screenshots in RAM only. Images are never stored on disk. User Telegram IDs are SHA-256 hashed before database storage. We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Designed in line with the principles of GDPR (EU) 2016/679, including data minimization, purpose limitation, and the right to erasure (see /delete).

Data We Collect

  • Hashed Telegram user ID
  • Game type selected
  • Timestamp of valuation
  • AI-generated valuation result

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Screenshots (processed in RAM, immediately discarded)
  • Account credentials or passwords
  • Payment details (handled by Telegram)
