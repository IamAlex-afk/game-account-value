What Actually Determines Value

What actually determines a PUBG Mobile account’s value?

  • Collection level — the game has 95 levels; every newly owned item adds points by its type and quality, so the level sums up the whole inventory.
  • X-Suits and their upgrade level — accounts with a max-level X-Suit asked €2,063 – €2,629 on FunPay.
  • Upgradable gun skins and the level each one reached (Glacier M416 is the one listings name most).
  • Supercar skins from car collaborations — listings count them one by one.
  • Rank adds little: it resets every season; on Eldorado.gg a “Gold Rank” account asked $1.99 and a “Diamond” one $3.75.

What should I look up before using the calculator?

  • The calculator asks for one number: the collection level from the Collection screen (for example 74.5).
  • The result is a rough range of public asking prices, not an offer. Buying or selling accounts breaks the game’s terms — see the rules.

Screenshots for an estimate

Which 5 screenshots does the bot need for PUBG Mobile?

PUBG Mobile spreads value over several inventory tabs, so the bot asks for five screenshots instead of three:

  • Profile — player ID, account level and rank readable.
  • Collection — the Collection screen with the collection level (for example 74.5). This one matters most.
  • Inventory → Outfits — X-Suits and Mythic outfits visible.
  • Upgradable gun skins — with each level (Lv.) visible.
  • Vehicle skins — supercars and other rare cars; the UC balance if it is shown.

Without the Collection screen the bot counts only what it can see — X-Suits, upgradable guns, supercars — and the estimate comes out lower. The bot never asks for a login or password, and screenshots are not stored.

Scams & Trading Risks

What are the biggest scams in PUBG Mobile account trading?

A PUBG Mobile account can be linked to several logins at once — for example Facebook, Twitter or Google Play. Any login the seller keeps is a way back in: the account is “recovered” after payment, and the buyer is left with nothing. Some FunPay listings are rentals by design (marked “Rent”), not sales. The publisher’s terms forbid transferring accounts at all, so neither side can turn to support when a deal goes wrong.

Rules & Trading Policy

Is trading PUBG Mobile accounts against the rules?

Yes. The PUBG Mobile terms (Proxima Beta Pte. Limited) say: “Your Account is personal to you and you are prohibited from gifting, lending, transferring or otherwise permitting any other person to access or use Your Account.” Virtual goods and currency may not be traded or sold either, and the publisher may suspend access or end the agreement.

Where do these listings come from?

Eldorado.gg, FunPay and igitems.com are international marketplaces, GameSatış is Turkish and GameTrade Japanese. Every figure on this page is an asking price seen by hand on October 14, 2026 — not a completed sale, and nothing was collected automatically.
