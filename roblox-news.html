Notable Findings

What's the biggest current risk, beyond price?

Account trading violates Roblox's Terms of Service

Buying, selling, or trading a Roblox account (as opposed to in-game items via official trading) is a direct ToS violation. Roblox monitors login-location and credential-change patterns and has been documented flagging transferred accounts for termination months after the sale — a real, current enforcement pattern, not a hypothetical.

Fake middleman scam

A scammer offers a "trusted middleman" (often a second account they control) to hold items or payment during a trade, then disappears with both sides' items. Roblox does not run or endorse any official middleman service.

Recovery scam

Targets people who already got scammed: fake "Roblox admins" offer to recover lost items or accounts for a fee — or for the victim's password. Always a scam; Roblox never needs your password to help you.

No verified record of an actual third-party Roblox account sale price was found. Widely cited "million-dollar" figures (e.g. Simoon68's ~$2.45M) are collector-holdings valuations of in-platform Limited items, not a documented resale transaction.
