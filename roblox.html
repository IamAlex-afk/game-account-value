How much is a Roblox account actually worth?

Marketplace range: $0.50 – $60 (up to ~$800 for specific experiences)

Roblox accounts do trade on third-party marketplaces despite Roblox's rules against it. On Eldorado.gg, live listings run from $0.50 (bare accounts from 2007-2015 with no notable progress) up to $59.99 for a clean 4-letter username. On igitems.com, accounts built around specific high-demand experiences — like a maxed "Pet Simulator 99" or a high-level Blox Fruits account — have listed as high as ~$800. Most listings that aren't tied to a specific rare experience sell well under $25.

Where do people actually buy and sell Roblox accounts?

Third-party marketplaces like Eldorado.gg and igitems.com host public account listings with visible asking prices, sorted by account age, username, Robux balance, and which specific game/experience the account is built around. This directly contradicts the idea that no marketplace exists — it's just less visible than, say, Steam's own trading system, because Roblox's Terms of Service prohibit it and platforms operate as third parties outside Roblox's control.

On a different platform (igitems.com), the single highest-value listing seen was a Pet Simulator 99 "end-game" account (~$800) — not confirmed as a repeat pattern within one platform's top 10, but the highest experience-specific figure found.

What is the Limiteds market, and how is a Limited actually priced?

Roblox has a separate, decade-old trading economy for "Limiteds" (cosmetic items no longer sold new) that runs almost entirely outside the account-sale marketplaces above. Rolimons.com, the community's standard tracker, defines three different numbers for the same item — mixing them up is the most common beginner mistake:

  • RAP (Recent Average Price) — a backward-looking average of actual recent trades. Easy to manipulate: a small group can trade an item back and forth at an inflated price to pump its RAP artificially.
  • Value — Rolimons' own forward-looking fair-trade estimate, which intentionally diverges from RAP when the community believes the RAP is being gamed.
  • Demand — a separate rating for how easily an item can actually be traded away. A high-RAP item with low Demand can be nearly impossible to offload at that price.

This is also the mechanism behind "projecting" — a named, real scam pattern where a holder deliberately trades their own item at an inflated price specifically to fake a high RAP and mislead less experienced traders. Off-sale status, genuine scarcity, and age (not RAP alone) are what Rolimons' own guidance says actually drives real trade value.

The single highest confirmed sale — as opposed to a current asking/tracker price — is Dominus Empyreus, sold for 69,000,000 Robux on June 24, 2022, cited consistently across three independent sources (Roblox Fandom wiki, RobloxDen, Playgama) as the platform's highest verified real-money-equivalent sale.

Converting Robux figures to USD is not straightforward: Roblox only cashes Robux out to real money through its DevEx program, at a fixed $0.0038 per Robux (raised from $0.0035 in September 2025), and DevEx requires verified-creator status, a minimum age of 13, and an active Premium subscription — an ordinary trader holding a Limited cannot simply redeem it 1:1 for cash. Treat these figures as in-platform trading value, not a guaranteed real-money payout.

What actually determines a Roblox account's value?

  • Which experience the account is built for — a maxed account in a currently-popular game (Pet Simulator, Blox Fruits, Adopt Me, Grow a Garden) sells for far more than a generic account with the same playtime
  • Discontinued Limited items — rare cosmetics no longer sold; some individually resell (via Roblox's own trade system) for real Robux value
  • Account age and OG status — usernames and registration dates from before 2010 carry collector cachet
  • Username quality — short (3-4 letter), dictionary-word, or number-free usernames are independently desirable and sell on their own
  • Robux balance and Premium membership history — liquid currency and a real subscription history signal an aged, legitimate account rather than a farmed one

How can I make my own account more valuable (without trading it)?

  • Hold onto Limited items rather than trading them away for short-term Robux — discontinued items only get scarcer
  • Keep Premium membership active continuously rather than letting it lapse and restart
  • Diversify across a few different Limiteds rather than concentrating value in one item, since Roblox has occasionally delisted or devalued individual items
  • Treat this as a collecting hobby with real risk, not an investment — Limited item values are illiquid and can swing hard

What are the biggest scams around Roblox accounts and items?

Account and item trading scams are extremely common in the Roblox community: fake "middleman" trade services, phishing sites that clone the Roblox login page to steal credentials, and "free Robux generator" scams that request account access. Because Roblox's own Terms of Service prohibit account transfers, victims of a trade scam have no recourse — Roblox will not restore a scammed account or reverse an item trade, and the scammed account itself remains banned if Roblox detects the unauthorized transfer.

Is it against the rules to buy or sell a Roblox account?

Yes. Roblox's Terms of Service explicitly prohibit transferring account ownership and trading accounts for real-world money. Accounts involved in detected transfers can be permanently suspended, with no appeal process for the buyer or seller.

Why do Roblox account prices look so much lower than other games?

Most Roblox accounts don't carry the same kind of "progress" that drives value in games like Clash of Clans or Free Fire — a Roblox account's value is concentrated in specific items, a specific experience's progress, or the username, rather than an overall level. That's why the bulk of listings cluster under $25, with a long thin tail of specific accounts (rare usernames, high-demand experience progress) reaching into the hundreds.
