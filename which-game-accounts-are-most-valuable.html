So does Minecraft actually have the most valuable accounts?

Only in one narrow sense: the rarest 2-digit numeric usernames, reported near $200,000, are the single highest number on this page. But that's a tiny collector niche — a normal Minecraft account with a normal username sells for $0.50-$632, cheaper than most of the other games' typical ranges. Minecraft is really two separate markets wearing one name, and the "$200,000" answer only applies to a handful of accounts that exist at all.

What's the highest ceiling among games with a genuinely broad, active resale market?

Genshin Impact's dedicated whale-account category, topping out at $8,995 for a 20×C6 account. Unlike Minecraft's username niche, this is part of a real, ongoing marketplace with regular turnover — Eldorado.gg runs it as a standing category, not a one-off listing.

Which game has the widest gap between "typical" and "outlier" prices?

Fortnite, by a wide margin. Typical live listings run $15-$300, but old historical reports (not current market listings) cite a single account at ~$90,000 tied to the Aerial Assault Trooper skin — a figure roughly 300x the current market's actual ceiling. This is the clearest example in our research of why "record sale" headlines from a few years ago shouldn't be trusted as a current price guide.

Does every game on this list actually have a real resale market?

Yes — including Roblox and Genshin Impact, which some sources describe as having no account marketplace at all. Every game covered here has at least one active third-party marketplace with real, visible prices, even where — like Minecraft's regular accounts or Roblox's non-username listings — the going rate is well under $25.

Which game's account value depends most on a single named item, rather than overall progress?

Fortnite, based on how concentrated the top listings are: 4 of the 10 highest-priced live listings we checked named the IKONIK skin specifically. Genshin Impact is close behind — Mavuika appeared at C6 in 5 of the 10 highest-priced whale listings. By contrast, Brawl Stars, Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, and Free Fire showed zero named-item concentration at all in their top listings — value there is purely about stats (trophies, Town Hall/King Tower level, rank).
